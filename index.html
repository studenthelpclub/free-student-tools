Crucial Tools for Form Filling (RTPS, SSC, UPSC)

Applying for State and Central government jobs requires strict adherence to digital formatting rules. Through our portal, you can execute the following tasks flawlessly:

  • RTPS Bihar Formatting: Creating Jati, Niwas, and Aay certificates requires a photograph merged perfectly with a signature. Our specialized Photo & Signature Merger automates this and compresses the final document under the mandatory 50KB limit instantly.
  • Identity Card Merging: Align, crop, and combine the front and back sides of identification documents into a single printable PDF or JPG for seamless verification uploads.
  • Exam Document Compression: Need to compress images to exactly 20KB or 50KB? Our Image Resizer allows you to target a specific KB output, making it perfect for SSC, Railway, and UPSC portal acceptance.
